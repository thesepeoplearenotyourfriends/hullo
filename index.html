    About

    This page creates a temporary conversation, not an account, contact, or message history.

    Temporary rendezvous servers help the two peers find each other. Messages and file contents are protected inside the established peer connection.

    Voice calls use WebRTC for realtime media transport while Hullo adds its own encryption to encoded call media before WebRTC transports it.

    Closing this page ends this browser’s conversation episode. A connection can fail when the networks cannot establish a usable peer path.
